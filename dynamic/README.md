# The dynamic case

`simulate.py` serves the calls of a period from the static fixed point, the cells moving as the load moves. Written by LBM #1 on 2026-09-30 and rewritten and first run by LBM #2 the same evening; the design is in `LBM/docs/decisions.md`.

The rules: the load of a dispatcher is the calls of the last fifteen minutes in its cell. A call is served by the closest free car in the caller's cell or an adjacent one; none: a car is invented at the call and counted. A car returns at the drop-off when the ride ends, and is lost if the drop-off is off the island. After a call, a dispatcher takes a turn of the static protocol (`../static/protocol.py`), at zero time, when it is discontent and its own load or a neighbour's has changed since its last turn --- by the call arriving, a call expiring, or a neighbour's step --- until no woken dispatcher has an admissible step, which is quiescence; a cap of `MAX_SWEEPS` sweeps guards against a cycle. Then the clock advances to the next call. Initial cars: one per call of the preparatory segment (the fifteen minutes before the start), placed at the call and riding to its drop-off; at the start it is free at the drop-off if the ride has ended on the island, and returns when it ends otherwise. Invented cars are counted from the start.

Sources in `../inputs/source/`: `lbm-trips-0800-0815.json` (the preparatory segment) and `lbm-trips-0815-0900.json` (the run), NYC Open Data yellow-taxi trips of 2015-01-15 with `pickup_datetime`, `dropoff_datetime`, `pickup_longitude/latitude`, `dropoff_longitude/latitude`; the transform to the simulator's kilometre frame is `../inputs/transform_lonlat_to_km.json`. The window at 08:15 from the trips file has 5,990 calls on the island against the 5,983 of the static run (the export's matched subset), and with run15's centres the loads differ by 7 over the 100 cells. `lbm-dropoffs-0800-0815.json` is not used: it was capped at 6,000 records by the query, and the initial cars come from the preparatory segment's calls instead (Udi, 2026-09-30).

Run, from this directory: `python3 simulate.py --from 08:15:00 --to 09:00:00 --out runs/dyn1` (the whole hour takes about 80 minutes in LBM #2's sandbox after the speed-ups of 2026-09-30 to `protocol.py`; the Cowork VM kills a process at 180 s, so a run there goes in chunks of `--max-calls` with `--resume`). Options: `--checkpoint N` calls between checkpoints (default 500), `--max-calls N` stop after N calls in this invocation, `--resume` continue from `<out>/state.json`, `--seed` (the order of the turns within a sweep).

Outputs in `runs/<name>/`: `log.jsonl` one line per checkpoint (calls, invented and lost cars, free cars and cars in a ride, content count, loads, calls after which quiescence was reached or the cap hit, moves, departures, splits, turns, mean/p50/p90 pickup distance in km, seconds per call); `calls.jsonl` one line per call (time, cell, pickup distance or invented, moves/departures/splits it caused, sweeps, turns, woken dispatchers, quiescent, content count, free cars, loads); `ckpt_HHMMSS.png` the map at each checkpoint, cells coloured by load as in the static runs, free cars as blue dots; `state.json` the full state at the last checkpoint, for `--resume`.

- `dyn1`: 08:15–09:00 from run15's fixed point, seed 1.
