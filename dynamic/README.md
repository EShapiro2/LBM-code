# The dynamic case

`simulate.py` serves the calls of a period from the static fixed point, the cells moving as the load moves. Written by LBM #1 on 2026-09-30 and rewritten and first run by LBM #2 the same evening; the design is in `LBM/docs/decisions.md`.

The rules: the load of a dispatcher is the calls of the last fifteen minutes in its cell. A call is served by the closest free car in the caller's cell or an adjacent one; when there is none it waits, and a car freed by a drop-off goes to the oldest waiting call whose cell or an adjacent cell, by the cells of that moment, contains it (Udi, 2026-10-01; until then a car was invented at the call and counted). The ride runs with its duration from the data, pickup at the call's time plus its wait, drop-off and the car's return shifted by the same wait; the wait is measured, no call is abandoned. A car whose drop-off is off the island is lost. After a call, a dispatcher takes a turn of the static protocol (`../static/protocol.py`), at zero time, when it is discontent and its own load or a neighbour's has changed since its last turn --- by the call arriving, a call expiring, or a neighbour's step --- until no woken dispatcher has an admissible step, which is quiescence; a cap of `MAX_SWEEPS` sweeps guards against a cycle. Then the clock advances to the next call. Initial cars: one per call of the preparatory segment (the fifteen minutes before the start), placed at the call and riding to its drop-off; at the start it is free at the drop-off if the ride has ended on the island, and returns when it ends otherwise. Beside each dispatch the closest free car anywhere at that moment is recorded, the optimal dispatch, which does not affect the dispatch.

Sources in `../inputs/source/`: `lbm-trips-0800-0815.json` (the preparatory segment) and `lbm-trips-0815-0900.json` (the run), NYC Open Data yellow-taxi trips of 2015-01-15 with `pickup_datetime`, `dropoff_datetime`, `pickup_longitude/latitude`, `dropoff_longitude/latitude`; the transform to the simulator's kilometre frame is `../inputs/transform_lonlat_to_km.json`. The window at 08:15 from the trips file has 5,990 calls on the island against the 5,983 of the static run (the export's matched subset), and with run15's centres the loads differ by 7 over the 100 cells. `lbm-dropoffs-0800-0815.json` is not used: it was capped at 6,000 records by the query, and the initial cars come from the preparatory segment's calls instead (Udi, 2026-09-30).

Run, from this directory: `python3 simulate.py --from 08:15:00 --to 09:00:00 --out runs/dyn1` (the whole hour takes about 80 minutes in LBM #2's sandbox after the speed-ups of 2026-09-30 to `protocol.py`; the Cowork VM kills a process at 180 s, so a run there goes in chunks of `--max-calls` with `--resume`). Options: `--checkpoint N` calls between checkpoints (default 500), `--max-calls N` stop after N calls in this invocation, `--resume` continue from `<out>/state.json`, `--seed` (the order of the turns within a sweep).

Outputs in `runs/<name>/`: `log.jsonl` one line per checkpoint (calls, served, waiting, lost cars, free cars and cars in a ride, content count, loads, calls after which quiescence was reached or the cap hit, moves, departures, splits, turns, the waits --- delayed calls, mean, median, p90, max, total --- and mean/p50/p90 pickup distance of the actual and the optimal dispatch in km with the count of non-optimal dispatches and the ratios, seconds per call); `calls.jsonl` one line per call, written once it is served, in call order (index, call time, cell, dispatch time, wait in seconds, pickup distance actual and optimal, moves/departures/splits the call caused, sweeps, turns, woken dispatchers, quiescent, content count, free and waiting at the call); `ckpt_HHMMSS.png` the map at each checkpoint, cells coloured by load as in the static runs, free cars as blue dots, waiting calls as red ones; `waits_HHMMSS.png` a bar chart of the waits by minute; `state.json` the full state at the last checkpoint, for `--resume`; `ckpt_end.png`, `waits_end.png` after the returns have served the last waiting call.

- `dyn1`: 08:15–09:00 from run15's fixed point, seed 1, under the rules of 2026-09-30 (a car invented when the neighbourhood has none), stopped and recorded at 08:22:34, 3,000 calls (Udi, 22:46).
- `dyn2`: 08:15–08:45, seed 1, under the rules of 2026-10-01 (a call waits for a car; the wait measured).
