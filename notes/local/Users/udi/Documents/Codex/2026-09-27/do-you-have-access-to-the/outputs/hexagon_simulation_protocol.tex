\section{Compact hexagonal cells: simulation protocol}
\label{sec:compact-hexagon-protocol}

The next experiment uses 100 cells. The earlier choice of 38 matched
the clustering baseline; it was not an optimized cell count.
Keep the same data, movement proposals, acceptance rule, and geometric
constraints. Use a finer initial mesh. The median score below controls
termination only, not acceptance of movements.

\paragraph{Data and coverage.}
Use the original 5,986 taxi pickups from 15 January 2015,
08:00--08:15, and the verified Manhattan main-island boundary with
its hole retained. Assign the 5,983 inside-domain records exactly once.
Retain and flag the three outside records. Record input identifiers,
boundary version, coordinate transform, and random seeds.

Let $D$ denote the island, $H_i$ an underlying convex six-sided cell
in the shared-vertex mesh, and $S_i=H_i\cap D$ its service region.
All 100 service regions must have positive area, cover $D$ completely,
and have no positive-area overlaps. Shared borders are allowed.
Use a documented deterministic assignment convention for pickups on
shared borders. Require, for every underlying and clipped cell,
\[
 \frac{\operatorname{diam}(A)}{w_{\min}(A)}\leq 2,
 \qquad A\in\{H_i,S_i\},
\]
where $w_{\min}$ is the minimum projection width over all directions.
Clipped service regions need not have six sides.
Repair initial geometry before balancing, without silently relaxing
the cell count, coverage, or shape constraints.

\paragraph{Median termination rule.}
Let $L_i$ be the load of cell $i$, and $N_i$ its neighbors sharing a
positive-length service-region border inside Manhattan. Point contact
alone does not establish adjacency. For $L_i>0$, define
\[
 g_i=\operatorname{median}_{j\in N_i}
       \left(100\,\frac{|L_i-L_j|}{L_i}\right),
 \qquad
 M=\operatorname{median}_{i=1,\ldots,100} g_i.
\]
Each percentage uses the focal cell's own load as denominator.
For an empty cell, set $g_i=+\infty$ if any neighbor has positive
load, and $g_i=0$ if all neighbors are empty. Report an empty neighbor
set as an invalid adjacency state rather than excluding that cell.
Include all 100 cells, including infinite scores. For an even number
of values, the median is the average of the two middle sorted values.

Report the initial score separately. Stop successfully at the first
completed balancing round with $M\leq5\%$. A manual stop, round cap,
time limit, or small movement does not establish convergence under
this rule. The median condition permits empty or poorly balanced
individual cells; it does not certify balance everywhere.

\paragraph{Test control before starting.}
Check that the execution environment works and that no unintended
duplicate solver is running. Demonstrate a short test that produces
a map, saves a resumable checkpoint, and stops on command.
Verify checkpoint retrieval and resumption before starting the full
experiment. Display the initial map before balancing.

\paragraph{Progress and snapshots every minute.}
During initialization, repair, and balancing, publish progress and a
saved map at least once per minute. Identify the run, timestamp,
stage, completed rounds, current score, best completed-round score
and its round, empty-cell count, and actual solver status.
Report coverage, overlap, assignment, and shape checks with their
audit timestamp; distinguish older audits from current measurements.
Map labels show loads. Captions distinguish initial, intermediate,
manually stopped, and converged states. If a snapshot is taken during
a round, label it and identify the last completed round used for the
termination score.

Use short interruptible work batches so that a long round or repair
operation cannot block reporting or stopping. Save consistent
checkpoints atomically, retaining geometry, ownership, random-number
state, update order, and the within-round position needed for exact
continuation. Keep timestamped snapshots and a latest checkpoint.
Monitoring reads saved state without restarting or duplicating the
solver.

\paragraph{Stop control and failure reporting.}
Provide a stop control checked between bounded work batches.
On request, preserve the latest consistent state, cease further
updates, and return a final map with confirmed process status.
Label manual stopping separately from convergence.
An active conversation indicator is not evidence of solver progress:
verify the process and advancing logs or checkpoints.
If execution, snapshot retrieval, or monitoring fails, report the
failure explicitly instead of claiming continued progress.
